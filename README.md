This is the **Claude Pocket** application for the [FoloToy AI Passport](https://ai-passport.folotoy.cn/en/),
an open, wearable ESP32-C3 device. Hold the button and talk; your answer comes
from Xiaoyou, a small ghost on the screen who can also hand work to other
agents and ask you before anything that needs your say. It connects over
encrypted Bluetooth LE to a phone companion app (or, without the app, straight
to the Claude desktop app in Developer Mode).
